# Company Hardware Warranty Policy

## 1. Warranty Coverage Period
All hardware products purchased directly from our authorized stores carry a standard 1-year limited warranty (12 months) starting from the confirmed date of purchase.
Extended 2-year or multi-year warranties are not offered or included in standard purchases.

## 2. Scope of Warranty Coverage
This warranty covers defects in hardware materials and manufacturing workmanship under normal consumer use and operating conditions.
If a hardware defect arises within the 1-year period, the company will, at its option:
1. Repair the hardware product using new or refurbished parts at no charge.
2. Replace the product with a functionally equivalent new or factory-recertified unit.

## 3. Explicit Exclusions
This limited warranty does NOT cover:
- Accidental drops, physical impact, crushed components, or cracked screens.
- Water damage, liquid immersion, spillages, or exposure to excessive moisture.
- Damage resulting from unauthorized repair attempts, disassembly, or third-party modifications.
- Normal cosmetic wear, scratches, dents, or battery degradation over time.

## 4. Replacement Programs and Tiers
All warranty claims follow our standard diagnostic and replacement procedure (5-10 business days).
The company DOES NOT operate any Premium, VIP, Platinum, or Same-Day free replacement program. All customer tiers and accounts are subject to the same standard 1-year limited warranty guidelines without exception.
