# Company Refund and Returns Policy

## 1. Return Window
Customers may request a return and refund within 30 days from the date of product delivery.
Return requests submitted after 30 days cannot be accepted or approved under any circumstances.

## 2. Product Condition & Eligibility Requirements
To be eligible for a full refund:
- The product must be in its original, unused, and undamaged condition.
- The item must include all original packaging, user manuals, cords, accessories, and warranty cards.
- The customer must provide the original receipt or proof of purchase order number.

## 3. Ineligible Items
The following categories of products are non-refundable and strictly ineligible for returns:
- Perishable goods and food items.
- Digital gift cards and downloadable software licenses.
- Custom-engraved or personalized merchandise.
- Products damaged due to customer misuse, neglect, or unauthorized alterations.

## 4. Return Shipping Fees
- For returns initiated due to customer preference, change of mind, or accidental ordering, a flat return shipping fee of $5.00 will be deducted from the refunded amount.
- If the return is due to a verifiable company fulfillment error or manufacturing defect, company will cover all return shipping costs.

## 5. Refund Processing Timeframe
Once the returned package is received and inspected at our central fulfillment warehouse (typically 2-3 business days), refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
